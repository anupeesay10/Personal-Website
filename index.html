About

Hello, I'm Anirudh ("Anu") Peesay, an independent researcher in mechanics focused on computational modeling of physical systems. I build models that simplify complex physics and engineering problems—especially those that are difficult or impossible to study experimentally. I graduated from the University of Pittsburgh in 2024 with a B.S. in Applied Mathematics, specializing in Mathematical Biology. I am currently interested in pursuing a master's degree in Mechanical Engineering, interested in doing research and eventual work as a Mechanical Engineer.
