About

Hello, I'm Anu Peesay, an independent modeling and simulation engineer in mechanics. As a modeling and simulation engineer, I build models that simplify complex mechanical engineering problems—especially those that are difficult or impossible to study experimentally. I graduated from the University of Pittsburgh in 2024 with a B.S. in Applied Mathematics, specializing in Mathematical Biology. I am currently a graduate student in Systems Engineering at University of Maryland, Baltimore County, and I am interested in working as a Systems Engineer in defense or aerospace.
